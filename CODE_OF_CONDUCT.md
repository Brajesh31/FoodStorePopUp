# Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, constructive, and inclusive participation.

- Be considerate and professional.
- Focus feedback on the work rather than individuals.
- Respect privacy and never post personal or payment information.
- Welcome questions and good-faith technical disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and doxxing are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community. Report serious incidents privately to the repository owner.
